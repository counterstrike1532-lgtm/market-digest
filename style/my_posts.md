1. Poland's KNF is considering new restrictions on retail CFD trading after Deputy Chair Dariusz Adamski revealed that 267,000 investors lost nearly PLN 2 billion in 2025. The announcement sent XTB shares down almost 19% at their lowest point.

So far, KNF has not published any draft parameters. The intervention could target leverage limits, retail access to CFDs, marketing rules, or how brokers manage client positions.

There is also an important distinction between investor losses and broker revenue. Under a market-maker model, a broker acts as the counterparty to client trades while hedging part of its exposure, so retail losses do not automatically translate 1-to-1 into broker income. Still, CFDs generated 90.5% of XTB's gross result on financial instruments (PLN 544.1 million) in Q3 2026, and Poland alone accounted for 59.1% of total revenue. That concentration leaves earnings directly exposed to tighter domestic rules.

What matters now is what KNF actually intends to target: how brokers acquire retail clients, or how they execute and manage their flow.
